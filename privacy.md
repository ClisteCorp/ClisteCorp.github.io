# Privacy Policy

**Application:** Cliste Corp Internal Bookkeeping Integration ("the App")  
**Publisher:** Cliste Corporation, a Delaware corporation ("Cliste Corp", "we")  
**Effective date:** October 2, 2026

## Who this policy covers

The App is a private, internal tool. It connects only to QuickBooks Online companies that Cliste Corp or its owner owns. It has no public users and collects no data from members of the public.

## What data the App accesses

With the authorization of the QuickBooks company's owner, the App reads the following through Intuit's QuickBooks Online Accounting API:

- company information (company name)
- the chart of accounts and classes
- profit-and-loss reports
- deposit and transfer records, used to reconcile property-management distributions

The App only reads data. It does not create, change, or delete anything in QuickBooks Online.

## How the data is used

The data is used only for the owner's internal bookkeeping, financial reporting, and tax preparation. It is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used for marketing.

## Where the data is stored

Data retrieved from QuickBooks Online is stored on computers and storage controlled by the owner. The OAuth credentials and access tokens that let the App connect are stored on the owner's local machine. They are not stored in source code or on shared storage.

## Sharing

Data is not shared with third parties, except with the owner's own tax and accounting advisors when needed for tax preparation, or when required by law.

## Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as it is needed for bookkeeping and tax recordkeeping, then deleted. The connection to QuickBooks Online can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the App in QuickBooks Online's apps settings. Once it is revoked, the App can no longer retrieve data.

## Security

Access to QuickBooks Online uses Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorization; the App never sees or stores QuickBooks passwords. Access tokens expire and are refreshed automatically, and credentials are kept out of version control.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
